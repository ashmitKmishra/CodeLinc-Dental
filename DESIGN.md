**Signature element: the Plan-Year Rail.** It is a timeline split by the reset line (Dec 31 | Jan 1). Care items sit on it as chips that move across the line when treatment is split across plan years.

Visual direction: a clear benefits statement with a friendly guide. Calm teal (`#006B72`), generous whitespace, honest numbers, restrained motion.
